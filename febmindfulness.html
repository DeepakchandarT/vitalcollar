Why we built this

We made the app we needed at 11pm, not 7am.

Most meditation apps are built for a version of you with a spare hour and a yoga mat. Febmindfulness was built for the version who's still in her work clothes, half-listening for the kids, and has exactly four minutes before the next thing.

Every session is recorded by a real teacher, in one take, without a script that sounds like it was written by a wellness brand. No gongs you didn't ask for. No shame about the days you skip.

"I stopped waiting to feel calm enough to meditate. Now I meditate because I don't." Naledi Osei, Head Teacher & Co-founder
Three ways in

Come in through whichever door is open.

Some days you need your breath. Some days your body. Some days just your thoughts, gently interrupted. Febmindfulness meets you at whichever one is closest.

Breath

Box breathing, box unpacking

Four-count patterns you can run under a desk, in a car, or on a call you've muted. Built to lower a racing heart in under ninety seconds.

Body

Slow scans, not stretches

Body-scan and progressive-release sessions for the tension you're carrying in your jaw, shoulders, and the back of your neck right now.

Mind

Thought-labeling, not thought-stopping

Guided noting practices for the 2am spiral and the mid-meeting worry. You're not asked to empty your mind — just to watch it more kindly.

How a session actually goes

Three unhurried steps.

No onboarding quiz that takes longer than the meditation. Open the app, and this is what happens.

01

Arrive

Tell it how much time you have, from two minutes to twenty. It picks a session that fits, not the other way around.

02

Settle

A teacher's voice, one take, no music you have to fade out mentally. You're guided, not narrated at.

03

Return

A soft chime, one line to jot how you feel, and you're back — steadier than you were four minutes ago.
