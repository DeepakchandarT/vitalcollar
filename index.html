Scene 02 — Sensing

Four channels. One continuous read.

Each zone is tuned to a distinct physiological signal, sampled continuously and passed forward for analysis.

Optical Sensing

Captures light-reflectance signals used to estimate cardiovascular rhythm.

Temperature Sensing

Tracks gradual shifts in skin-adjacent temperature over time.

Motion Sensing

Detects movement, posture change and activity intensity.

Respiratory Monitoring

Observes breathing cadence and rhythm consistency.
