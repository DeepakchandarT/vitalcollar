Your privacy matters.

Privacy Policy · FebFit Wellness Hub

Updated 16 October 2026 · Notice version 2026-10-16

The essentials

You can browse without completing a questionnaire. Enquiry details are sent only when you complete the form and agree to their use. An optional photo is analysed in your browser; the photo is not sent with your enquiry. Sharing calculated photo observations is a separate, optional choice.

1. Who this notice covers

This notice describes the FebFit Wellness website and its coaching enquiry and optional photo snapshot features. For questions about your information, contact the FebFit team using the contact details below. External services have their own privacy notices.

2. Information used and why

InformationPurpose and destination
Contact detailsYour name and telephone number are included in your enquiry so the team can respond and discuss coaching.
Questionnaire answersAge, profession, city, fitness goal, training location, preferred session length and movement concerns are used to prepare an initial summary and inform the coaching discussion. These details are sent to the configured Google Apps Script / Google Sheets enquiry service when you submit.
Optional photoThe selected image is held temporarily in the page and processed locally by MediaPipe. It is not included in the form submission. No camera access is requested.
Optional photo observationsA small set of image angles and landmark-visibility information stays in the page unless you tick the separate sharing checkbox. If selected, the calculated report accompanies your enquiry, without the image or raw landmarks.
Consent and submission detailsA submission identifier, timestamp, notice version and your consent choices accompany the enquiry.
Technical requestsThe hosting provider and external resource providers receive ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address, browser information and requested resource, when serving pages, images, fonts or model files.
Wellness enquiriesYour name, contact number and requested support are sent to the same enquiry service to arrange follow-up.
Mood check-insYour selected mood and the date are saved in this browser to display your weekly check-ins and streak. The current site does not send these entries to the enquiry service.

3. Your photo and AI snapshot

The optional feature uses AI to locate landmarks, then calculates a few two-dimensional image observations. It does not determine body fat, waist circumference, health, physical balance or an injury. It does not provide a diagnosis or decide whether you qualify for care. The current site does not send photos for model training.

Choose only a photo you have permission to use. This enquiry and photo feature is for adults aged 18 or over. Keep the photo optional and avoid including other people or identifying documents.

4. External services

Google Apps Script / Sheets receives submitted enquiries. Google Fonts serves fonts. jsDelivr and Google-hosted MediaPipe resources provide the image-analysis software and model. The updated home images are served with this website. Your photo is not part of these resource requests.

Opening WhatsApp takes you to a separate service; messages you choose to send there are governed by its policies. These providers may process information in countries other than yours. See Google’s privacy policy and WhatsApp’s privacy policy for their practices.

5. Storage and retention

Questionnaire answers and the selected photo remain in this page’s memory while you use it. Removing a photo clears its preview and calculated report; reloading the page clears the current questionnaire and photo. The site does not intentionally save photos to browser storage.

If an enquiry fails, your entries remain in the open page so you can retry. This version does not save an enquiry queue or send automatic background retries. Reloading clears those entries. Mood selections are different: they persist in this browser until you clear site data or use the control below.

Submitted enquiries are held separately in the team’s Google enquiry system. This page cannot confirm a server-side deletion schedule or erase those records automatically. Contact the team to ask about retention or request deletion. Clearing your browser does not delete an enquiry already received by the team.

6. Choices, access and requests

  • Browse without submitting the questionnaire.
  • Skip the optional photo, remove it, or leave photo-report sharing unchecked.
  • Before submission, close or reload the page to discard the current form.
  • Contact the team to request access, correction or deletion of your submitted information, withdraw consent for future use, or raise a privacy complaint. Include the contact detail used in your enquiry so the team can locate it; do not send identity documents unless a necessary verification method has been agreed.

Withdrawal does not undo a request already sent. Some records may need to be retained where applicable law requires it; ask the team for the reason and relevant period. Applicable privacy rights and complaint routes depend on your location and the law in force.

7. Cookies and local controls

This version does not add advertising cookies or an analytics tracker. External providers and browser settings may still affect caching and requests. The button below removes saved FebFit mood check-ins and any legacy enquiry queue on this browser only; it does not delete server records or information in other open tabs.

8. Security and delivery

Avoid entering information that is not needed for your enquiry. No website can guarantee absolute security. The form’s cross-origin delivery mechanism cannot confirm that an enquiry was received; contact the team if you need confirmation.

9. Contact and changes

Use the existing FebFit contact number +91 90258 63098 for privacy requests, or open a WhatsApp conversation. No message is sent automatically.
